### Q2 2026 Earnings — Record Quarter (Aug 10, 2026)
- **Revenue: $234.1M** (+62% YoY; beat consensus of ~$231.6M)
  - Product revenue: $181.3M / Service revenue: $52.7M

### Iridium Acquisition — Landmark Deal (Announced July 3, 2026)
- Rocket Lab to acquire **Iridium Communications** in a historic deal creating a fully vertically-integrated space powerhouse (design, build, launch, operate constellations)
- Estimated deal value ~$8B
- **Progress updates (Aug 13, 2026):** HSR period lapsed; Form S-4 filed; FCC applications filed; capital strategy underway
- **Concerns:** CEO Peter Beck's planned $465M stake reduction via 10b5-1 plan (~$286M sold in a recent week) is a near-term sentiment overhang

### Mynaric & Motiv Space Systems Acquisitions — Closed
- **Mynaric** (inter-satellite laser communications) — closed by Q2 end
- **Motiv Space Systems** (Mars-proven robotics) — completion announced May 26, 2026
- These strengthen vertical integration: RF/laser comms + robotics for on-orbit servicing

### GHOST — Globally-Deployable Launch System (Aug 10, 2026)
- New containerized launch system enabling suborbital & orbital launches from anywhere
- First location: **Rocket Lab Launch Complex 4** at Pacific Spaceport Complex, Kodiak, Alaska (two pads)
- Operational debut: suborbital launch from Alaska in **2027**

### Rocket Lab Germany GmbH (Aug 10, 2026)
- Formal establishment to deliver sovereign space capabilities to Europe
- CEO Peter Beck calls Europe his next "actionable" market

### Neutron Launch Deal with Kepler Communications (Aug 10, 2026)
- Dedicated Neutron mission for Kepler — first time Kepler has booked an entire rocket for constellation deployment (rideshare → dedicated)
- Launch NE**T no earlier than 2028**, deploying multiple satellites to LEO with optical connectivity, on-orbit compute, and hosted payload capacity

### $397M Space Force SB-AMTI Contract (Aug 4, 2026)
- Awarded to build and launch multiple **Flatellite** spacecraft on Neutron for the U.S. Space Force's Space-Based Airborne Moving Target Indicator program
- Rocket Lab is **one of only two vendors** delivering launch-plus-spacecraft for SB-AMTI — validation of vertical integration strategy
- Mission: detect, track, and monitor airborne threats from space

### Record $266M Missile Defense Contract (July 27, 2026)
- Largest launch contract in company history — multi-launch HASTE suborbital launches with U.S. Space Force for missile defense

### iQPS Multi-Launch Deal (July 30, 2026)
- Third multi-launch booking on Electron by iQPS in less than a year — three dedicated missions for SAR Earth-imaging constellation

### Electron Launch Cadence
- **92nd Electron mission completed** (Aug 6, 2026) — 13th Electron launch of the year, 8th for iQPS
- Electron regularly achieving 100% mission success on consecutive launches

## 📌 Bottom Line

Rocket Lab is executing at a record pace: **Q2 revenue $234M (+62% YoY), backlog $2.36B, EBITDA losses narrowing faster than guided.** The company is rapidly transitioning from a pure-play launch provider to a **fully vertically-integrated space infrastructure company** — building satellites, operating constellations (via Iridium acquisition), providing laser/RF comms (Mynaric), robotics (Motiv), and launching on Electron, HASTE, and soon Neutron.

The **single most important catalyst** remains the **Neutron maiden flight (Q4 2026)** — a successful debut would re-rate the stock by proving Rocket Lab can compete in the medium-lift market. Near-term sentiment is pressured by CEO insider selling ($465M plan), the Iridium deal's execution risk, and broader space-sector volatility, but analyst consensus remains solidly bullish with average targets around $110.
