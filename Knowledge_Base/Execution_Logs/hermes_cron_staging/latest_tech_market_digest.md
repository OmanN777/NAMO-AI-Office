## Key News Wrap-Up

### 1. TSMC — AI Chip Demand Remains the Anchor
- TSMC reported **Q2 profit up 77.4% YoY**, beating estimates, with HPC now ~66% of revenue (5nm: 33%, 3nm: 30%).
- Raised **2026 revenue and capex guidance** — capex now **$60B–$64B** (up from ~$41B in 2025), signaling multi-year AI buildout confidence.
- CEO C.C. Wei: "AI is real" — dismissed bubble fears; AI-related revenue CAGR expected in **high-50% range through 2029**.
- Stock closed at **$429.89** (+1.86%), near the upper end of recent range.
- **EMIB-like packaging tech** reportedly in development to compete with Intel; NVIDIA interested for future 4-GPU packaging.
- **NVIDIA–TSMC fab AI partnership:** TSMC is deploying NVIDIA CUDA-X, cuLitho, cuEST, cuML, Metropolis, and Omniverse FabTwin across lithography, defect inspection, process control, and fab optimization — a 20–50% cost/cycle-time improvement in computational lithography.
- **Arizona expansion:** TSMC committing additional **$100B** to accelerate U.S. fab buildout (CFO Wendell Huang, July 20). NVIDIA Blackwell chips already in production at Phoenix plants; packaging/testing via Amkor and SPIL. Total U.S. investment approaching $150B+.

### 2. NVIDIA — $500B Infrastructure Financing + Rubin Roadmap
- NVIDIA announced partnerships with **Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR** to mobilize **$500B+** in third-party capital for AI compute infrastructure (Aug 10 press release).
- **Analyst reactions:** BofA calls it a positive first step that shifts funding burden off NVIDIA's balance sheet; compute as "investable asset class" hinges on CUDA-driven residual value. Wedbush sees it as reinforcing NVIDIA's leadership away from hyperscalers (~50% of revenue).
- Reported **$3B Lancium investment** (Stargate Texas campus) — not yet formally confirmed.
- Jensen Huang revealed **"Rubin"** next-gen AI chip architecture, slated for 2026 release following Blackwell.
- Stock: closed **$224.09** (−0.18%), essentially flat after a strong prior week. Intraday saw −2.86% at one point on profit-taking. Stock jumped ~2.5% on the news day (Aug 10), up ~1% on Aug 11.
