# Salesforce (CRM) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
Salesforce is pivoting aggressively from traditional SaaS to an "Agentic Enterprise AI" platform. Their Agentforce 360 platform aims to automate entire workflows rather than just providing CRM tools.

### 2. Fundamentals
While topline growth has matured compared to its hyper-growth phase, CRM is a cash flow machine. They have a massive $25 billion buyback program and declared a dividend, signaling a shift toward robust EPS growth. 

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/CRM/2026-06-08-news.md`, early June saw stock volatility due to cautious revenue guidance. However, the rollout of "Summer '26 Release" (Multi-agent orchestration), the appointment of a new AI-focused President (Rohan Kumar), and a $5B stake in Anthropic highlight strong future AI integration.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** If Agentforce successfully monetizes at scale, CRM transitions from a software tool to an essential autonomous AI worker provider, justifying a multiple re-rating.
**Bear:** The transition to AI might cannibalize traditional seat-based software licenses, leading to sluggish immediate revenue growth.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Exit the position if the Anthropic partnership fails to integrate seamlessly into Customer 360, or if enterprise adoption of Agentforce falls significantly below peer AI offerings (like MSFT Copilot).

### 6. What to ask
"Are the short-term revenue guidance fears an overreaction creating a buy-the-dip opportunity for our aggressive portfolio?"
