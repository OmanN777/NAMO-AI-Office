# Shopify (SHOP) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
Shopify remains the premier e-commerce infrastructure platform. They are aggressively integrating AI to boost merchant conversion rates and simplify ad creation, acting as the operating system for modern retail.

### 2. Fundamentals
Q1 2026 revenue was $3.17 billion (up 34% YoY) with GMV hitting $101 billion. However, Q2 guidance was cautious (high-twenties % growth). The company has a strong balance sheet, reinforced by a massive new $5 billion share repurchase authorization.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/SHOP/2026-06-08-news.md`, despite a Q1 post-earnings selloff, early June catalysts include a new $3B buyback expansion and an expanded advertising partnership with Reddit to drive merchant growth.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** The 13x increase in orders from AI-powered searches proves their AI integration is driving real ROI for merchants. The stock dip and heavy buybacks create an attractive entry point.
**Bear:** Macroeconomic headwinds and high interest rates could pressure consumer spending, slowing GMV growth.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Liquidate if Q2/Q3 revenue growth drops below 20% YoY, or if the newly authorized $3B buyback program fails to support the stock price amidst deteriorating e-commerce macro conditions.

### 6. What to ask
"Does the massive $5B total buyback authorization provide a strong enough floor for us to initiate a high-growth position?"
