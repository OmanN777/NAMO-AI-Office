# EQT Corporation (EQT) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
EQT is a major U.S. natural gas producer. As AI data centers strain the electric grid, natural gas is increasingly viewed as the necessary bridge fuel to ensure reliable power generation when renewables fall short.

### 2. Fundamentals
Highly sensitive to commodity prices. The company is actively managing production curtailments to support prices and focusing on midstream integration to lower transport costs. 

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/EQT/2026-06-08-news.md`, analysts have a neutral outlook in early June 2026. The focus is on their ability to capture regional gas demand from data centers and LNG exports. Paid a $0.165 dividend on June 1.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** If nuclear energy expansion is too slow to meet AI power demands, natural gas peaker plants will be the only viable solution, driving EQT's volume and pricing power up.
**Bear:** Vulnerable to natural gas gluts and heavy near-term capital spending.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Sell if natural gas prices collapse and stay depressed due to oversupply, or if data centers successfully bypass gas entirely in favor of dedicated nuclear/geothermal.

### 6. What to ask
"Does EQT fit our high-tech aggressive growth thesis, or is it too dependent on unpredictable macro commodity cycles?"
