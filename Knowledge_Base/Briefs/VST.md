# Vistra Corp (VST) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
Vistra is an integrated retail electricity and power generation company. It has transformed into an AI-darling by utilizing its nuclear and natural gas assets to provide the 24/7 baseload power data centers desperately need.

### 2. Fundamentals
Huge cash generation. Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA hit $1.49 billion. They achieved investment-grade credit ratings and have high institutional ownership (~91%).

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/VST/2026-06-08-news.md`, the stock had a slight pullback in early June 2026 after massive 5-year gains. They are currently integrating the Cogentrix natural gas portfolio acquisition. 

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** They hold the keys to the AI revolution: reliable electricity. Analysts see prices moving to $225–$233. 
**Bear:** The stock has already run up 7x over five years; much of the AI power thesis might already be priced in. Commodity price volatility remains a risk.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Exit if AI hyperscalers stop signing long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) with Vistra, or if natural gas/nuclear generation costs spike uncontrollably.

### 6. What to ask
"Has the easy money already been made in VST over the last 5 years, or is the AI data center rollout going to drive another multi-year rally?"
