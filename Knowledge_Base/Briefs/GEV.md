**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
A powerhouse in power infrastructure (gas turbines, wind, electrification). They are perfectly positioned to upgrade the aging global electrical grid to handle the AI era.

### 2. Fundamentals
The Electrification segment is exploding, with orders up 86% in Q1 2026 to $7.1 billion, and a massive $42 billion backlog.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/GEV/2026-06-08-news.md`, GEV is expanding aggressively in India (100 MW wind farm deal). However, the stock pulled back 11% in May/June due to offshore wind legal complexities and CEO insider selling.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** The $42 billion backlog guarantees revenue for years. Upgrading the grid is non-negotiable for AI expansion.
**Bear:** Insider selling (CEO sold shares) and legal hurdles in the offshore wind segment are causing short-term bearish technical crossovers (10-day crossing below 50-day MA).

### 5. Kill Conditions
Exit if the Electrification backlog begins to shrink, or if legal liabilities from legacy wind projects severely damage free cash flow.

### 6. What to ask
"Can we overlook the insider selling and bearish technicals to grab a piece of the $42B backlog at a discount?"
