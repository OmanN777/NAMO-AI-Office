# Brookfield Renewable (BEPC) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
BEPC is a global leader in renewable power assets (hydro, wind, solar). While not a tech stock, it provides the critical, scalable infrastructure (power) needed to fuel the massive energy demands of AI data centers.

### 2. Fundamentals
Strong cash generation and capital recycling. Q1 FFO increased 19% YoY. They offer a reliable and growing dividend ($0.392/share), acting as a stabilizer in a high-risk portfolio.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/BEPC/2026-06-08-news.md`, BEPC stock is up ~38% YTD through early June. They recently issued C$150M in preferred units to fund higher-returning projects.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** Surging global demand for energy security and AI data centers directly benefits their diversified renewable portfolio. 
**Bear:** Highly capital-intensive. Rising or sustained high interest rates could increase debt servicing costs and pressure the stock's yield appeal.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Sell if the FFO growth stagnates (drops below 5% YoY) or if major tech companies successfully bypass utilities by building their own dedicated off-grid nuclear/SMR solutions.

### 6. What to ask
"Does BEPC provide enough 'Aggressive Growth' for our portfolio, or is it purely a defensive dividend play to offset our high-risk tech exposure?"
