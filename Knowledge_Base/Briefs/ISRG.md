# Intuitive Surgical (ISRG) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
The undisputed monopoly in robotic surgery. A highly scalable razor-and-blades model where hospitals buy the expensive robot (da Vinci) and continuously purchase high-margin instruments for every surgery.

### 2. Fundamentals
Stellar fundamentals despite stock price action. Q1 2026 revenue rose 23% YoY to $2.77 billion. The new da Vinci 5 platform is seeing massive adoption with 11% higher utilization rates.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/ISRG/2026-06-08-news.md`, the stock has crashed 26-28% YTD down to the low $400s. The decline is driven by a valuation reset, fears of Chinese competition, and irrational speculation about OpenAI entering robotics. Japan just authorized reimbursement for 7 robotic surgeries.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** The market has irrationally punished a fundamentally flawless monopoly. The da Vinci 5 supercycle is just beginning, making this 28% drop a generational buying opportunity.
**Bear:** Hospital CapEx freezes in China and intense new competition could compress historical margins permanently.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Liquidate if da Vinci 5 adoption stalls, or if a competitor (like Medtronic) actually proves they can steal double-digit market share in the US.

"Is this 28% YTD drop the ultimate 'blood in the streets' opportunity for a high-conviction aggressive growth play?"
