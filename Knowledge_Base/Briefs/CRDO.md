# Credo Technology (CRDO) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
Credo provides high-speed connectivity solutions (Active Electrical Cables, DSPs) crucial for scaling AI data centers. They are the "picks and shovels" of the AI networking infrastructure layer.

### 2. Fundamentals
Hyper-growth territory. Q4 2026 revenue jumped 157% YoY to $437 million. Full-year revenue tripled. EPS beat estimates comfortably.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/CRDO/2026-06-08-news.md`, despite massive earnings beats on June 1, the stock dropped 10-12% due to impossibly high market expectations. They also recently acquired DustPhotonics to boost their AI factory tech.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** Shares surged 78% in three months before earnings. The post-earnings dip is a classic "sell the news" event on a fundamentally flawless growth story. 
**Bear:** The stock might be overvalued even after the dip. Any slowdown in hyperscaler networking CapEx will crush the multiple.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Sell if revenue growth drops below 50% YoY, or if competitors (like Marvell or Broadcom) introduce superior, cheaper interconnect standards that obsolete CRDO's AECs.

### 6. What to ask
"Does this post-earnings 10% drop present an aggressive buying opportunity, or is the market signaling a top in networking multiples?"
