# Constellation Energy (CEG) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
Constellation Energy is the largest producer of carbon-free energy in the US, primarily through its massive nuclear fleet. It is uniquely positioned to supply the massive, reliable "always-on" baseload power required by hyperscale AI data centers.

### 2. Fundamentals
Highly profitable with strong cash flows driven by long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) with major tech giants. 

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/CEG/2026-06-08-news.md`, the stock suffered a pullback in early June 2026 due to an 11 million share secondary offering at $281/share. However, operationally, they just completed a 25MW geothermal expansion at The Geysers to support California's clean energy goals.

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** The AI revolution cannot happen without massive nuclear power. The recent secondary offering dip provides an excellent entry point into a structural monopoly-like asset.
**Bear:** Nuclear regulatory hurdles (like the NRC assessments) take time. The stock is highly volatile and sensitive to block sales.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Exit the position if next-generation energy storage (e.g., solid-state batteries) makes intermittent renewables cheaper and more reliable than nuclear baseload, drastically lowering CEG's pricing power.

### 6. What to ask
"Is the recent dip from the secondary offering the exact technical entry point we've been waiting for to ride the AI-energy supercycle?"
