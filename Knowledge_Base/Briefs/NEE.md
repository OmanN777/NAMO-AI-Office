# NextEra Energy (NEE) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
NextEra is the world's largest producer of wind and solar energy. It combines a highly regulated utility (FPL) with a massive, fast-growing clean energy business (NextEra Energy Resources).

### 2. Fundamentals
A massive 33 GW renewable energy backlog. The recent acquisition of Dominion Energy will create the world's largest regulated electric utility and accelerate EPS growth to >9% annually through 2032.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/NEE/2026-06-08-news.md`, the stock is up 5.3% YTD, outperforming peers. The Dominion deal allows them to capture the explosive data center power demand specifically in Virginia (the data center capital of the world).

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** The Dominion acquisition is a masterstroke to capture AI power demand directly at the source (Virginia). The 9%+ EPS growth makes it an elite utility.
**Bear:** Utility stocks are highly sensitive to interest rates; if rates stay "higher for longer," it pressures utility valuations and debt costs.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Sell if regulatory bodies block or severely alter the Dominion Energy acquisition, or if the renewable backlog faces mass cancellations.

### 6. What to ask
"Does the Dominion acquisition transform NEE from a defensive utility into a viable AI-infrastructure growth play?"
