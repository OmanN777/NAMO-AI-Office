# NVIDIA (NVDA) - Research Briefing
**Prepared by:** Earnchan & Fundamentokung
**Date:** 2026-06-08

### 1. Snapshot
NVIDIA is the undisputed king of AI infrastructure. Their GPUs and networking solutions are the backbone of the global generative AI revolution. Highly scalable and benefiting from massive recurring hardware/software enterprise demand.

### 2. Fundamentals
Revenue growth is astronomical. Q1 2027 revenue hit $81.6 billion (up 85% YoY), with Data Center revenue specifically surging 92% to $75.2 billion. Margins remain elite, supported by aggressive $80B stock buybacks.

### 3. Latest Earnings
Based on `sources/NVDA/2026-06-08-news.md`, the Q1 2027 report crushed expectations. Furthermore, recent strategic deals in South Korea (SK Hynix, Naver) and a £1.7 billion AI infrastructure investment in the UK (Nebius) show massive ongoing global deployment. 

### 4. Bull/Bear Case
**Bull:** AI is just starting. "Agentic AI" will require exponentially more compute. CEO Jensen Huang considers recent tech sell-offs a "buying opportunity."
**Bear:** Valuation multiples are historically high. Interest rate volatility could compress P/E ratios, and competitors (AMD, custom ASICs) are attempting to chip away at market share.

### 5. Kill Conditions
Sell or flip to short if Data Center revenue growth drops below 30% YoY for two consecutive quarters, or if hyperscalers dramatically reduce CapEx spending due to an AI monetization failure.

### 6. What to ask
"How much of the future growth is already priced into the current multiple, and do we wait for a deeper tech correction before adding to our position?"
